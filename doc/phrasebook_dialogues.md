## 2. Starport Trade & Operations Dialogue

### Scenario:
A merchant vessel docks at Outpost Kepler-42. The dockmaster communicates with the freighter captain.

```
Dockmaster:
"Halo, kapten. Wat kargo yu bring tu Kepler-42?"
(Hello, Captain. What cargo do you bring to Kepler-42?)

Captain:
"Wi bring ten tauzend ton of kristals and nu medisin."
(We bring ten thousand tons of crystals and new medicine.)

Dockmaster:
"Gud. Ku yun enaji is saf?"
(Good. Is your power/energy safe?)

Captain:
"Yes, all sistems is saf and operating."
(Yes, all systems are safe and operating.)

Dockmaster:
"Pliis mov-up de ramp, den mov-in tu bay faiv."
(Please lift [raise] the ramp, then move in [enter] to bay 5.)

Captain:
"Wi andastan. Wi wil mov-in now."
(We understand. We will move in [enter] now.)
```

## 3. Commercial Negotiation

```
Buyer:
"Wat is de prais for dis wata purifikator?"
(What is the price for this water purifier?)

Seller:
"De prais is faiv hondred kredits."
(The price is five hundred credits.)

Buyer:
"Dis is tu mac. Yu kan sel it for for hondred?"
(This is too much. Can you sell it for four hundred?)

Seller:
"Mi kan sel for for hondred and fifti. It is mor gud dan de old model."
(I can sell for 450. It is better than the old model.)

Buyer:
"Agri. Mi bayed it. Mi giv de kredits now."
(Agreed. I buy [accept] it. I give the credits now.)
```
