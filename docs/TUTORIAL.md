# Univerlan — Beginner's Crash Course

Welcome to **Univerlan**, the rationalized interstellar auxiliary language designed for clear, robust, and unambiguous communication.

---

## Lesson 1 : The Bijective Alphabet & 1:1 Spelling

In Univerlan, spelling is 100% phonetic. Every sound has exactly one letter, and every letter has exactly one sound:
- No silent letters: `knight` $\to$ `nait`, `doubt` $\to$ `daut`.
- No double letters: `apple` $\to$ `apel`, `better` $\to$ `beter`.
- Pure vowels:
  - `a` as in *father* (`kat` = cat)
  - `e` as in *bed* (`bed` = bed)
  - `i` as in *machine* (`sip` = see/ship)
  - `o` as in *dock* (`dok` = dock)
  - `u` as in *flute* (`lun` = moon)
- Distinct digraphs:
  - `c` is always [tʃ] (*check* $\to$ `cek`)
  - `x` is always [ʃ] (*ship* $\to$ `xip`)
  - `j` is always [dʒ] (*job* $\to$ `jop`)

## Lesson 2 : Pronouns & Possession

Personal pronouns are streamlined:
- `mi` = I, me
- `yu` = you (singular & plural)
- `hi` = he, him
- `xe` = she, her
- `it` = it
- `wi` = we, us
- `dey` = they, them

### Possession Made Simple: Add `-n`
Simply attach `-n` to any pronoun:
- `min` = my, mine (`min xip` = my ship)
- `yun` = your, yours
- `hin` = his
- `xen` = her, hers
- `win` = our, ours
- `deyn` = their, theirs

---

## Lesson 3 : Zero Irregular Verbs

Forget irregular past tenses. In Univerlan, **every single verb** forms its past with `-ed`:
- `mi go` $\to$ `mi goed` (I went)
- `mi bay` $\to$ `mi bayed` (I bought)
- `mi tek` $\to$ `mi teked` (I took)
- `mi is` $\to$ `mi ised` (I was)

Future is formed with particle `wil`:
- `mi wil go` = I will go.

---

## Lesson 4 : Wilkins Relational Vectors

Instead of memorizing dozens of arbitrary words for movement, Univerlan uses directional kinetic vectors based on John Wilkins' philosophical principles:
- `mov-up` = lift, elevate (move upward)
- `mov-don` = drop, fall (move downward)
- `mov-in` = enter (move inside)
- `mov-ut` = exit (move outside)
- `mov-tu-mi` = pull (move toward speaker)
- `mov-fro-mi` = push (move away from speaker)

*Example:*  
`Plis mov-up de kargo.` = Please lift the cargo.

## Lesson 5 : Starport Conversation

* **Captain** : `Halo! Hu is yu?`  
  *(Hello! Who are you?)*
* **Pilot** : `Min nem is Kael. Mi is pilot of de xip.`  
  *(My name is Kael. I am the pilot of the ship.)*
* **Captain** : `Plis mov-in de staxon vek. Wi nid fud vek.`  
  *(Please enter the station quickly. We need food quickly.)*
* **Pilot** : `Mi understanded. Mi wil goed nau.`  
  *(Understood. I will go now.)*
